Roadside Acres: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Roadside Acres ("the game") is made by Shawn Riley ("we"). This policy explains what information

the game handles and why.

What we don't collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we don't run any servers. Your farm is saved only on

your device. We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded videos, which you choose to watch for in-game rewards. They're

provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • your approximate location (from your IP address),
  • information about the ads you see and tap,
  • basic diagnostics about your device.

In the EU, UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can change your choice at any time under Menu → Land → Privacy choices. On iPhone, the game

doesn't ask to track you, so ads aren't personalized with your advertising identifier.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see

your payment details. The game keeps a record of your purchase receipts on your device so that each

purchase is delivered once and can be restored.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when crops

are ready). They're created on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's

settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information

from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes your farm and everything saved with it. Menu → Land → Erase save

also wipes it. To reset the advertising identifier, use your phone's privacy settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
